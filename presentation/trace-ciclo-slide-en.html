The full picture

Five stages, a cycle that closes

Each stage has a concrete deliverable and an exit criterion — we don't move to the next one until it's met. We follow the same illustrative case (Andina Retail) throughout the whole cycle.

T

Target

Translate the business problem into a measurable outcome.

Deliverable
Prioritized use cases + KPI + baseline
ExampleWhatsApp agent for order status. KPI: % resolved without a human (0% today).
Gate: a use case with owner, KPI, and estimated value
R

Read

X-ray data, architecture, risks, and feasibility.

Deliverable
Assessment report with gaps by perspective
ExampleLegacy ERP with no API: High gap in Platform. Data needs anonymizing (Law 21.719).
Gate: feasibility confirmed (data + architecture + compliance)
A

Architect

Design the target solution, the roadmap, and the cost.

Deliverable
Architecture + roadmap + costs + PoC plan
ExampleBedrock + Lambda + DynamoDB. ~USD 800/month. Success criterion: ≥70%.
Gate: cost approved, PoC scope agreed
C

Craft

Build iteratively and test the PoC hypothesis.

Deliverable
Working PoC + evidence vs. success criteria
Example3 sprints, 200 conversations tested: 74% resolved. Decision: go.
Gate: documented go/no-go decision
E

Evolve

Production, measuring results, and continuous improvement.

Deliverable
Traceability artifact: objective vs. measured result
Example71% in production at 2 months: +71 points vs. 0% baseline.
Gate: positive delta or a documented action plan

Evolve closes the loop against the objective defined in Target: the measured result is always compared against the original baseline. That traceability is ZirconTrace's signature.

The TRACE cycle
Stage 1 of 5
T

Target

We translate the business problem into a measurable outcome: prioritized use cases, with an owner, KPI, and baseline.

TRACE
You get

A short document, no technical jargon: the central problem, prioritized use cases (owner, KPI, baseline, estimated value), and the service package that best frames the starting point.

What we do
  • Interviews with business and process owners.
  • Use-case prioritization by value and effort.
  • KPI definition and baseline measurement.
Gate

There's at least one use case with owner, KPI, and estimated value — confirmed in writing before Read starts.

Illustrative example (fictional) · Andina Retail
Problem
The customer support team is getting 40% more WhatsApp inquiries than last year; average response time is 6 hours.
Use case
A WhatsApp support agent for order status and return inquiries.
Owner
Head of Customer Experience.
KPI / baseline
% of inquiries resolved without human intervention — baseline 0% (100% human today).
Estimated value
Reduce the CX team's workload by 25%.
Output Use case approved, ready for the Read assessment.
Target
Stage 2 of 5
R

Read

We x-ray the current state — data, architecture, risks, feasibility — before designing anything.

TRACE
You get

Gaps by CAF perspective, a review against the 6 pillars of Well-Architected, the state of your data, and your exposure under Law 21.719 if you operate in Chile.

What we do
  • Inventory of data sources and quality.
  • CAF Assessment + Well-Architected Review.
  • Map of regulatory and security risks.
Gate

Feasibility confirmed — data, architecture, and compliance — before moving to Architect.

Illustrative example (fictional) · Andina Retail
Technical finding
Order data lives in a legacy ERP with no API — a High severity gap in the CAF Platform perspective.
Data
18 months of historical conversations available, enough to evaluate the agent.
Compliance
Law 21.719: purchase history needs to be anonymized before it's used to train the agent.
Conclusion
Feasibility confirmed, conditional on resolving ERP data access before Architect.
Output Assessment signed off with 1 open condition and its owner.

Architect

We design the target solution, the roadmap, and the cost — before we build, not after.

TRACE

Gate

Estimated cost approved and PoC scope agreed, with measurable success criteria.

Architecture
Amazon Bedrock (Claude) + Lambda + DynamoDB, integrated with the WhatsApp Business API.

Integration layer over the legacy ERP to expose order status as an internal API.
Estimated cost
~USD 800/month in infrastructure (sa-east-1 region).
Success criterion
≥70% of inquiries resolved without escalating to a human, measured across 200 real conversations.
Output 3-sprint PoC approved with budget and criteria signed off.

Stage 4 of 5
C

Craft

We build iteratively and test the hypothesis against the success criteria defined in Architect.

TRACE
You get

Testing evidence per environment, each success criterion with its result, a closing demo, and an explicit, documented go/no-go decision.

What we do
  • Short sprints with a demo at the end of each one.
  • Agent evaluation against real data.
  • Result logged per success criterion.
Gate

Every success criterion has a recorded result and there's a documented continuity decision.

Illustrative example (fictional) · Andina Retail
Build
3 sprints. PoC tested against 200 historical conversations and validated live with 20 real customers.
Result
74% of inquiries resolved without human intervention — beats the success criterion (70%).
Learning
Return inquiries need more context: they're left out of the initial scope.
Decision
Go to production, approved by the Head of Customer Experience.
Output Go/no-go decision documented, with evidence attached.
Craft
Stage 5 of 5
E

Evolve

Production, measuring results, and continuous improvement — this is where we close the loop against the objective defined in Target.

TRACE
You get

The traceability artifact: Target's objective, baseline, KPI measured in production, and delta. Cost dashboards, and an evolution plan.

What we do
  • Going live and KPI observability.
  • FinOps review of real vs. estimated cost.
  • Opening the next cycle with a new Target.
Gate

Positive delta, or if not, explained with an action plan — and at least one evolution item for the next period.

Illustrative example (fictional) · Andina Retail
2 months live
Measured KPI: 71% of inquiries resolved without intervention (0% baseline).
Delta
+71 points vs. baseline — slightly below the PoC's peak (74%), explained by the wider variety of real-world inquiries.
Real cost
USD 740/month vs. the USD 800 estimated in Architect.
Evolution plan
Add an email channel and open a new Target cycle for automated returns.
Traceability Target's objective → result measured in production.
