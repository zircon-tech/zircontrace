How we work

The TRACE cycle

Every technical decision, traceable from the business objective to the measured result. We didn't invent new stages: we anchor on AWS Well-Architected, the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework, FinOps, and Working Backwards, and we add what no consultancy our size makes explicit, that the final outcome can be traced, step by step, back to the business decision that started it.

T

Target · Translate the business problem into a measurable outcome

We leave with prioritized use cases, each with an owner, a KPI, and a baseline.

Deliverable
Opportunity 1-pager + KPIs and baseline
Gate
A use case with owner, KPI, and estimated value
R

Read · X-ray the current state

Data, architecture, risks, and feasibility, using AWS Well-Architected Review and AWS CAF as our lens.

Deliverable
Assessment report with gaps by perspective
Gate
Feasibility confirmed: data, architecture, and compliance
A

Architect · Design the solution, roadmap, and cost

Reviewed against the 6 pillars of Well-Architected, with cost estimation done before we build.

Deliverable
Architecture + roadmap + cost estimate + PoC plan
Gate
Cost approved, PoC scope agreed
C

Craft · Build iteratively

We test the hypothesis against success criteria defined upfront in Architect.

Deliverable
Working PoC + evidence vs. success criteria
Gate
Documented go/no-go decision
E

Evolve · Production and continuous improvement

We measure the real result against the objective defined in Target. That comparison is ZirconTrace's signature.

Deliverable
Dashboards + traceability artifact (objective vs. result)
Gate
Positive delta or a documented action plan

Evolve closes the loop against the objective defined in Target: it's the traceability promise no consultancy our size makes explicit.
