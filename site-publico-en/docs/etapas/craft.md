**What it's about:** we build iteratively and test the hypothesis against the success criteria defined in Architect. Without that, there's no honest way to say whether the proof of concept worked.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You get** | Testing and quality evidence across every environment; each success criterion defined in Architect, with its result (meets, doesn't meet, or partial); a closing demo; a record of what was adjusted during the build to lower cost; a prioritized backlog of what's left for production; and an explicit, documented decision to continue or not. |
| **Stage closed when** | Every proof-of-concept success criterion has a recorded result; there's a documented continuity decision; and the production backlog is prioritized and shared with you. |
