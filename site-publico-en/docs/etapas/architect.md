**What it's about:** we design the target solution, the roadmap, and the cost, before we build, not after.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You get** | An architecture diagram with the key design decisions justified; an explicit checklist against the 6 pillars of Well-Architected; a phased roadmap (what gets built in the proof of concept and what's left for later); a month-by-month infrastructure cost estimate, service by service; the proof of concept's success criteria, explicit and measurable; and a quote. |
| **Stage closed when** | The architecture is reviewed against the 6 pillars of Well-Architected; the estimated cost is approved; and the proof of concept's scope is agreed, with measurable success criteria. |
