**What it's about:** we translate the business problem into a measurable outcome. We don't leave this stage with a list of ideas: we leave with prioritized use cases, each with an owner on the client side, a KPI that moves, and a baseline to measure against later.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You get** | A short document, no technical jargon: the central problem in one or two sentences, prioritized use cases (owner, KPI, baseline, and estimated value for each), the service package that best frames the starting point, the risks and assumptions you identified, and next steps if there's a green light. |
| **Stage closed when** | There's at least one use case with a defined owner, KPI, and estimated value; the central problem is written without technical jargon; and you confirmed in writing that these are the use cases to evaluate in Read. |

Without this, we don't start Read: there's no point x-raying something without knowing what we're looking for.
