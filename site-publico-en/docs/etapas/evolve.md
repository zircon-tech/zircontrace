**What it's about:** production, measuring results, and continuous improvement. This is where we close the loop: we measure the real result against the objective we defined in Target. That comparison is ZirconTrace's signature.

## The traceability artifact

A fixed-format table, not a sales narrative:

| Objective defined in Target | KPI and baseline | KPI measured in production | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| the original objective | the starting number | the real measurement | +/- % |

If the delta isn't positive, we explain it and put together an action plan — we don't hide it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You also get** | KPI and real infrastructure cost dashboards; maintenance and support plans with different levels of monthly capacity, depending on what you need; and an evolution plan with suggested next steps, which may open a new Target cycle. |
| **Stage closed when** | The traceability artifact is complete, without staying as loose narrative; the measured KPI uses the same metric as the Target baseline (or it's explained why it changed); and there's at least one evolution-plan item for the next period. |
