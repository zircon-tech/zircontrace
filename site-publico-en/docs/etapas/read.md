**What it's about:** we x-ray the current state (data, architecture, risks, feasibility) before designing anything. We use AWS Well-Architected Review and the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) as our lens, plus an explicit check of regulatory exposure.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You get** | An assessment report: gaps by CAF perspective (Business, People, Governance, Platform, Security, Operations) with severity; a review against the 6 pillars of Well-Architected; the state of your data for the chosen use case; your exposure under **Law 21.719** if you operate in Chile or process personal data there; and a clear conclusion on whether it's viable to move to Architect. |
| **Stage closed when** | Feasibility is confirmed: data, architecture, and compliance. Every gap identified has an assigned severity. You received the report and confirmed next steps. |

!!! note "Law 21.719 (Chile)"
    In effect since December 1, 2026. Fines of up to 20,000 UTM, or 4% of your revenue in case of repeat violations. During the first year in effect, only warnings apply to SMBs.
