# How we work

ZirconTech is an AWS Advanced Tier Partner, with offices in Montevideo and New York. We help organizations adopt AWS, modernize existing platforms, optimize costs, and build data, machine learning, and generative AI solutions on native AWS services.

## What sets us apart

- **Clear, fast communication**: we adapt to the client's channels, with mechanisms for timely response.
- **Control over deliverables**: agile methodologies, a transparent development process.
- **On-time delivery**: planning with detailed requirements and teams that adapt to the project's profile.

## Collaboration models

We work with a dedicated team, fixed price, or time & materials, depending on what best fits the project.

## Our process

1. Strategic design
2. Analysis and prototyping
3. UI/UX design
4. Development
5. Testing and QA
6. Deployment and launch

This process is the "how" behind the Craft and Evolve stages of the TRACE cycle.
